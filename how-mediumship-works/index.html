    Understanding Mediumship — The Bridge Between Worlds

    Mediumship is the ability to perceive and communicate with the consciousness of those who have passed. Here is how it works and what the evidence shows.

    What Is Mediumship?

    Mediumship is a practice in which a trained individual — a medium — acts as an intermediary between the living and the deceased. The medium perceives information from the spirit world through various channels and relays it to the person seeking connection.

    This practice has existed across cultures and centuries. From ancient oracles to modern evidential mediums, the core principle remains the same: consciousness survives physical death, and communication across that threshold is possible.

    How Mediums Receive Information

    Clairvoyance (Clear Seeing)

    The medium receives visual impressions — images of the deceased person, symbolic visions, scenes from their life, or visual representations of messages.

    Clairaudience (Clear Hearing)

    The medium hears words, names, phrases, or sounds associated with the communicating spirit. This might include a name, a pet phrase, or a significant word.

    Clairsentience (Clear Feeling)

    The medium feels the emotions, physical sensations, or personality characteristics of the spirit. They might sense a particular health condition, an emotional state, or a distinctive personality trait.

    Claircognisance (Clear Knowing)

    The medium simply knows specific information without a clear sensory channel. Facts, names, dates, or circumstances arrive as direct knowledge.

    Evidence-Based Mediumship

    At Medium Reading, we practice evidential mediumship. This means the medium provides verifiable information to confirm who is communicating before delivering messages. Evidence might include:

    • Physical appearance and distinguishing features
    • Personality traits and mannerisms
    • Specific shared memories
    • Names of people and places
    • Significant dates or anniversaries
    • Cause or circumstances of passing
    • Current events in the sitter’s life that the spirit acknowledges

    This evidence-first approach ensures that messages are received with confidence and meaning.

    What Mediumship Is Not

    • It is not fortune-telling — mediums connect with the deceased, not predict your future
    • It is not a performance — genuine mediumship is a sensitive practice, not entertainment
    • It is not guaranteed to produce specific results — the spirit world cannot be commanded
    • It is not a replacement for grief counselling — it is a complement to the healing process
