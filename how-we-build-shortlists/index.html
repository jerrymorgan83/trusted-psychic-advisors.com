How we build our reader shortlists

Trusted Psychic Advisors helps you compare the information in reader profiles. These are editorial shortlists, not a test of psychic accuracy.

What a reader card tells you

Names, portraits, specialties and reading styles come from saved provider profiles. The underlying reference was preserved on October 9, 2026; individual fields may have been collected earlier. We use the original profile photograph and link to the provider so you can confirm the details.

How we choose a shortlist

We group profiles by their stated topics and include a mix of communication styles. A profile must have a recorded source and a usable photograph. The numbered order helps readers navigate the list; it is not a score, league table or claim that one person has stronger abilities.

What we have not verified

We have not carried out test readings, identity screening or background checks on these advisors. We do not independently confirm their claimed abilities. Descriptions of clairvoyance, mediumship and similar practices are the providers’ own descriptions.

Prices and availability

Rates, offers, communication formats and schedules change. We deliberately send you to the provider for current details rather than presenting an old price or an online indicator as live information.

Commercial links

Reader cards link to the source profile saved in our reference. Those links do not add an affiliate tracking code. Other pages on this website may contain commercial links and should be read together with their disclosures.

Your decision comes first

Readings are for personal reflection and entertainment. They do not replace medical, legal, financial or mental health advice, and cannot guarantee reconciliation or another person’s choices.
