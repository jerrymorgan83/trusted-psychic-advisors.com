Psychic Source Review (2026): Pricing, Guarantee and Honest Pros and Cons

Disclosure: Trusted Psychic Advisors may earn a commission if you click a link and buy a service. This never changes how we score a platform. Psychic readings are for entertainment and personal reflection only and are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or mental health advice. This review is based on published information and public user reviews; we do not claim to have personally tested the service. See how we review.

Verdict: Psychic Source is one of the oldest names in the business, with phone, chat, video and offline-messaging options, an app, a stated screening process and a published satisfaction guarantee. Public reviews are mixed, with complaints about vague readings and promo codes, and promotional prices can differ from everyday rates.

Best for: people who prefer phone readings and want a published guarantee on their last paid reading.

Overview and history

Psychic Source states that it has been operating since 1989, and its FAQ refers to more than thirty-five years in business. That makes it one of the earliest names in telephone-based psychic services, predating the internet marketplaces by a decade or more. Longevity is not a measure of accuracy, but it does suggest the company has survived multiple shifts in technology and regulation.

The company presents itself as a curated service. It says only about two percent of psychics who apply are accepted, and that screening includes multiple test readings, background verification and an assessment of abilities, with performance monitored through member feedback. We cannot audit these claims. What they signal is positioning: a managed roster rather than an open listing.

It ranks sixth in our list, behind Medium Reading, Kasamba, California Psychics, Keen and Purple Garden. The lower position reflects public feedback on consistency and promo-code problems, not a lack of features; on paper, it has one of the most complete service menus here. How Psychic Source works

Account and purchase

The homepage says that to begin you create an account and make a purchase. This is the typical prepaid structure, in which you buy time or credit and then spend it. Read what happens to unused balance, and whether the promotional rate applies to the first purchase only.

Finding an advisor

You select an advisor by specialty, such as tarot or relationships, or use the site’s “Find a Psychic” tool. The site lists advisor types including career advisors, clairaudients, clairsentients, clairvoyants and psychic mediums, and specialties including couples, fortune tellers, love, pet and twin flame readings. Reading types range from angel cards, astrology and cartomancy to dream interpretation, lost object, numerology, past life, soulmate and tarot.

Choosing a channel

You can connect by phone, chat or video call, or use a mini reading. If the advisor you want is offline, the platform offers messaging. That variety is a genuine strength, particularly for people who prefer a phone conversation to typing. The contact phone number for support is displayed on the site.

Minimums

The homepage did not state minimum session lengths in the visible text. Check at checkout, and note whether promotional pricing applies only to certain lengths or advisors.

Pricing and the intro offer

The homepage advertises psychic readings “as low as $0.60 per minute,” available online one time only with a promo code, and separately advertises readings “as low as $1 per minute.” A bottom banner promotes 50 percent off with another code. We do not reproduce the codes here because they change, and we recommend reading the live terms instead.

Standard per-minute rates beyond these promotional figures are not shown on the page. That matters, because promotional rates usually apply to a limited block of time, after which the advisor’s regular rate applies. Public reviewers have reported that a promo code offered to them did not work, so confirm that the code is applied before you start your session, and take a screenshot of the confirmation.

A sensible approach is to treat the promotional rate as a trial, then check the advisor’s standard rate before deciding to continue. Our article on free psychic reading offers explains how this works across platforms.

Check before you pay: promotional rates are one-time and time-limited. Confirm the current price and the rate that applies after the promotion ends.

Satisfaction guarantee and refunds

The site describes a satisfaction guarantee and a risk-free guarantee on your last paid reading if you are not satisfied. If you feel an advisor is not connecting, the site says you can end the session and contact customer support. The homepage does not spell out eligibility, time limits or the process. A public excerpt of the company’s policy on Trustpilot describes unused time being returned, up to twenty minutes of the time back, to try the service again. We could not confirm that wording on the homepage, so read the current guarantee page.

Note that this policy, as described, appears to return time rather than cash. That is consistent with how Kasamba handles unsatisfactory readings. If a straightforward cash refund matters to you, ask support what is possible before you buy.

Practical tip: if a reader is not connecting with you, say so politely and end the session quickly. Waiting until the end of a long call to complain makes your case harder to evaluate. Read how to get an accurate psychic reading for more.

How to pick a reader on Psychic Source

We do not name individual advisors. Rosters rotate and we cannot verify that any person is currently available at a given price. The following steps work on any platform.

  1. Start with the specialty. Use the listed advisor types and specialties to narrow your search, for example love, couples or career.
  2. Try the Find a Psychic tool. It can reduce overload if you are not sure where to begin.
  3. Read reviews carefully. Platforms control how reviews are displayed. Look for consistent comments on the same advisor, and weigh specific accounts above one-word praise.
  4. Check the rate and the promotion. Confirm the rate that applies after the promotional block ends.
  5. Choose the format that suits you. If you dislike typing, a phone call may be more comfortable than chat.
  6. Use a short first session. Pose one clear question. If the reply is vague or scripted, which is a common complaint about readers on many platforms, move on.

Be alert to pressure tactics, such as being told that your situation is urgent or that only an additional paid session can prevent harm. For more warning signs, see Are psychics real?

Specialties offered

This platform has one of the more detailed category menus. Beyond mainstream love and career readings it lists pet readings, lost objects, past life work, twin flame and soulmate readings, angel cards and dream interpretation. Twin flame and soulmate content is popular but also the area where unrealistic promises are most common, so be extra sceptical there. Our article on whether a psychic can tell if an ex will come back is a good primer.

The site lists psychic mediums, but if contact with someone who has died is your main reason for seeking a reading, a specialist may suit you better. Medium Reading is the top-ranked alternative in our list. It is focused on mediumship, and its own site states a free first question with no card required, though it does not publish prices.

App and user experience

The company lists an app on both the Apple App Store and Google Play. That is useful if you plan to use the service often, and it gives you another place to read user reviews before you commit. An app also makes it easy to start a session impulsively, so keep a budget in mind and consider disabling one-tap purchases if your device allows.

The website is built around a clear set of entry points: choose a specialty, use the finder, or call. The offline messaging option is a thoughtful touch, because it lets you contact an advisor who is not currently available. The main usability caution is the interplay between promo codes and checkout, which public reviewers have flagged.

Privacy and safety

The company says it screens advisors with multiple test readings and background verification. If true, that is a reasonable baseline, but it does not protect you from poor advice. Keep your own safeguards: do not share bank information, passwords or identification numbers, and avoid giving more detail about third parties than you are comfortable with.

Review the privacy policy for retention of call, chat and video data. Use a payment card with good dispute protection. If you are experiencing a crisis, speak with a qualified professional or emergency service, and be wary of any advisor who discourages you from doing so.

What public reviewers say

Trustpilot snapshots for Psychic Source show scores that vary by regional page and date, from roughly the mid-threes to about four out of five, based on several hundred to nearly a thousand reviews. We cannot give a definitive live number, so consult the page itself.

  • Vague or scripted readings. Some reviewers say the readings felt scripted or generic.
  • Poor matches. One reviewer reported trying several advisors, including one who said they were not picking up anything and suggested seeking a refund.
  • Promo code problems. At least one reviewer said a code they were offered did not work.
  • Profit motive. Some anonymous reviewers allege that advisors are primarily interested in extending sessions. These are opinions, not verified findings.
  • Company responses. Trustpilot notes the company had not replied to negative reviews at the time of the snapshot.
  • Praise. Other reviewers describe particular advisors as gifted, compassionate and helpful, and an automated Trustpilot summary suggested most reviewers were somewhat happy overall.

As always, satisfaction depends heavily on the individual advisor. Use public reviews to spot patterns, and do not rely on a single number.
