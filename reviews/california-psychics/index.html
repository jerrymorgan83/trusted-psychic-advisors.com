Overview and history

California Psychics tells visitors that it has been in business since 1995, which makes it one of the older names in the phone and online reading industry. The homepage cites more than thirty years of experience and describes a screening process in which only two out of every hundred applicants are accepted. As with all such figures, these are the company’s own statements. We cannot verify the application numbers, but the positioning is clear: this is a curated roster, not an open marketplace where anyone can list.

That distinction is the main reason people choose it. On an open marketplace you decide who is worth your time from a very large list. On a curated service the brand has already filtered the list, and you decide among a smaller number of vetted readers. Whether the filter produces better readings is something no outside reviewer can prove, but it does reduce the chance of landing on someone with no track record at all.

California Psychics is third in our ranking, behind Medium Reading and Kasamba. Medium Reading is first because of its mediumship focus and free first question, as stated on its site. Kasamba is slightly ahead because of its wider visible choice and lower entry rates. To see all six compared, go to our main comparison page.

How California Psychics works

Choosing how to start

The homepage offers two paths. You can choose a reader yourself by topic, such as love, career, tarot or medium, or you can use a “Match Me” option that suggests someone for you. If you are new to readings and unsure how to evaluate profiles, the matching option can reduce choice paralysis, though you should still read the profile you are offered.

Connecting

Sessions are available by phone and by chat. The homepage lists a toll-free number and says the service is available around the clock. A company app is also offered, with live access to readers. If you use the app, check the permissions it requests and the privacy policy it links to.

Account and payment

You will need an account to start. The pages we reviewed did not state minimum session lengths or list every accepted payment method in the visible text, so confirm these at checkout. As always with pay-by-the-minute services, the clock matters: you pay for time, not for answers.

The first-reading offer

The homepage links to “15 minutes free” for first readings. In previous years, this brand has run different introductory structures, such as a discounted per-minute rate for a block of minutes, so the exact mechanics can change. Read the terms of the live offer, including how the free minutes are applied, whether a card is required and what the rate becomes afterwards. Our article on free psychic reading offers explains what to look for.

Pricing and the intro offer

The homepage has a pricing link but does not display rates in the text we could read. Third-party roundups give conflicting figures, which is itself a sign that rates vary by reader and change over time. One guide lists a broad range from about two dollars to fifty dollars per minute, with most readers between roughly two and five dollars. Another cites entry rates from around four dollars and a range up to a little over eight dollars. We cannot reconcile these, and several of those sources earn commissions, so we recommend you ignore the quoted numbers and check the live profile.

What we can say is that this brand is generally positioned above bargain-bin pricing. Public reviewers frequently describe it as expensive. A fifteen-minute call at five dollars a minute would be seventy-five dollars, so plan your budget with a realistic range rather than the lowest headline rate.

The first-time offer reduces the risk of a first session. Use it to test the style of one reader, then decide whether paying full rates for more time makes sense. If you are cost-sensitive, set a hard limit in advance and end the session when you reach it.

Check before you pay: the pricing page and live profiles are the only reliable source for current rates. Offers and promotional terms change without notice.

Satisfaction policy and refunds

The homepage says that if you are not satisfied, you can contact customer care within twenty-four hours and the company will work to resolve the issue. It also says the company does not guarantee that predictions will come true. We did not find on the page a description of a formal refund process or a fixed credit amount, so the policy is best read as a customer-care commitment rather than a clear money-back guarantee.

The twenty-four-hour window is the most practical detail. If a session went badly, contact support the same day, explain what happened in specific terms, and keep a record of the time, the reader’s name and what was discussed. Vague complaints are harder to resolve than concrete ones.

Also note the line that the service is for entertainment purposes and for adults aged eighteen or over. That is the legal framing for the whole industry, and it is a reason to treat any reading as a prompt for reflection and not as professional guidance. How to pick a reader on California Psychics

We do not name individual readers. Rosters change, and we cannot verify who is currently listed or what they charge. Instead, use this checklist.

  • Begin with the topic filter. Love, career, tarot and medium categories are shown on the homepage. Pick the one that matches your question.
  • Consider the Match Me tool if you are unsure. It is a quick way to see a reasonable shortlist, but compare it against at least two profiles you choose yourself.
  • Read the written reviews. Look for detail on how the reader communicates, whether they ask good questions and whether they are honest when they are unsure. Treat uniformly glowing text with some caution, since some public reviewers say not every negative review is displayed on company pages.
  • Compare rates within a category. A higher rate should not be treated as proof of better quality.
  • Prepare one clear question. Open questions like “What does my future hold?” invite generic answers. Specific questions let you judge usefulness.
  • End the call if you feel pressure. A reader who warns of danger unless you keep paying is not acting in your interest.

Specialties offered

The homepage groups readers by topic including love, career, tarot and mediums. A roster of this kind generally includes clairvoyants, clairsentients, tarot readers and mediums. The brand also lists general psychic readings and relationship guidance. If your primary question is about reaching someone who has died, there are mediums on the roster, but the platform is not exclusively focused on that field.

If mediumship is your main reason for seeking a reading, you might prefer Medium Reading, our top-ranked option for contact with loved ones. It concentrates on that specialty and, per its own site, offers a free first question without a card. For broader topics, California Psychics offers a reasonable spread.

App and user experience

The company states that an app is available for live access to readers. That is a convenience if you intend to use the service regularly, but it also makes it easier to keep a session running longer than planned, so keep an eye on the timer. The website is straightforward, with topic entry points and a prominent phone number. Compared with open marketplaces, there is less clutter because the roster is smaller.

The support number is displayed prominently, and the stated commitment to contact within twenty-four hours is helpful. We have not tested response times and make no claim about them.

Privacy and safety

The screening claim is the main safety feature the platform highlights. We cannot audit how screening works, but a curated roster usually means fewer unvetted individuals. Even so, apply your own rules: never share bank information, passwords or sensitive identity data, and be careful about repeating details of other people in your life.

Review the privacy policy for what the service records or retains, especially if you use chat or the app. Use a payment card with strong dispute protections. If you are in distress, a reading is no substitute for qualified help, and any reader who discourages you from seeking such help should be avoided.

What public reviewers say

On Trustpilot, California Psychics has shown a score of roughly four out of five across several thousand reviews, with the exact number differing by regional page and capture date. We cannot present a precise live figure, so check the page itself.

Common themes in public feedback include:

  • Price. The most frequent negative theme is cost, with some reviewers calling the service very expensive and describing short stretches of useful content for the money.
  • Inconsistent accuracy. Some reviewers found readings vague or inaccurate. This varies by reader.
  • Availability of strong readers at certain times. A few reviewers felt that the choice at some hours was thin.
  • Handling of negative reviews. Some reviewers object to how critical feedback is displayed on company pages.
  • Customer service. Many reviewers praise support staff as kind and quick to resolve issues, which offsets some of the criticism.

As with any review platform, a self-selected sample is not a scientific survey. Read a spread of one-star and five-star reviews and look for patterns, not outliers.
